I Stand With British Jews

A quiet campaign of solidarity. On Tisha B’Av 2026 I asked non-Jewish Britons to wear a simple badge bearing the Union Flag and the words “I Stand With British Jews”.

No marches. No slogans about foreign policy. Just a small act of neighbourly decency.

Covered by The Times and Jewish News. The son of an IRA killer turned informer knows something about fanaticism — and why ordinary people need to show ordinary solidarity.
