I Stand With British Jews

Today and tomorrow · 4 and 5 October 2026

Wear your badge

The Green Party conference in Brighton has voted that Zionism is racism: 1,022 for, 813 against. Members also rejected a safeguard for Jews who belong to a synagogue that calls itself Zionist. Outside the hall, people chanted that Zionists were not welcome.

If you have a badge, wear it today and tomorrow. Not as a foreign-policy statement. As a neighbour’s answer. British Jews still have friends in Britain. They still belong here.

Notes from today

4 October 2026 · afternoon

For those of you who own an I Stand With British Jews badge: this is the day to wear it. Let’s show the Greens that, despite their best efforts, British Jews still have friends in Britain. Still belong here. Still have a place in their home, and ours.

4 October 2026 · the vote

Conference adopted the motion and committed the party to a single Palestinian state “in all of historic Palestine”. Four of the party’s five MPs had opposed it. Deputy leader Rachel Millward called the wording problematic. It passed anyway. The party’s own statement is here. The BBC report is here.

4 October 2026 · morning, while they were still debating

Since Oliver Cromwell allowed Jews to settle again in England, Britain has been a refuge — imperfect, but an island redoubt in the worst of times, compared with its European neighbours. A major party has now decided that the ordinary Jewish belief in a Jewish state is racism. Why do we never learn the lessons of history?

The company that slogan keeps

A quiet campaign of solidarity. A badge with the Union Flag and the words I Stand With British Jews. Today and tomorrow, especially.

No marches. No rallies. No foreign-policy slogans. Just a small, visible act of neighbourly decency.

10 pence from every badge sold goes directly to AJEX – The Jewish Military Association.

AJEX

AJEX represents Jewish men and women who have served, or are still serving, in the British Armed Forces. Around 50,000 British Jews served in the First World War; more than 70,000 in the Second.
