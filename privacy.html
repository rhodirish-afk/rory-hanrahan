Privacy

This is a personal static website for Rory Hanrahan. It is hosted on GitHub Pages.

What this site does not do. There is no account system, no analytics cookie wall, and no mailing-list form on this domain that stores your details. Pages are ordinary HTML.

Contact and RSVP. If you email myone_ie@hotmail.co.uk (for example to RSVP to Veterans’ Story Hour, or about The First Pint), that correspondence is handled in ordinary email. Do not send sensitive personal data you would not want in an inbox.

Haters / screenshots. The “Haters of the Week” corner may publish screenshots of public comments. If you send material for that purpose, assume it may appear on the site. Ask before sharing anything that identifies a private individual in a harmful way.

External links. Links to Substack, X, Facebook, Ko-fi, newspapers and other sites are subject to those sites’ own privacy practices.

Hosting. GitHub (and visitors’ browsers/CDNs such as the Tailwind CDN) may process standard technical request logs. See GitHub’s privacy documentation for Pages hosting.
