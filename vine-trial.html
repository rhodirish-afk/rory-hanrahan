Still pouring after 35 days

A real-world trial of Freshkask at The Vine Inn — and why it matters for the future of cask ale.

29 July 2026 · By Rory Hanrahan

The Vine Trial · flipbook

Open full screen

See the flipbook and pubs page for the full trial narrative. Two Jennings beers in Freshkask vessels went on 24 June 2026; one was still pouring in superb condition on 29 July — thirty-five days later — through a heatwave and a failed cellar.

The sealed system can protect beer far beyond the normal cask window. For quieter pubs and clubs that want real ale without constant wastage, that matters.

The trial, in brief

Filled 18 June · On the bar 24 June · Still pouring 29 July

Beers: Jennings Back Yam and Cocker Hoop (4.6%). Official window: 14 days. One vessel still superb at 35 days.
