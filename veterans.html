Veterans’ Story Hour

A new charity bringing British Armed Forces veterans into pubs, schools and community centres to share stories of service, resilience and comradeship.

Supported by a Civic Future Action Grant. Starting in Oxfordshire with the aim of going national.

The idea is simple: put veterans in ordinary rooms with ordinary people, and let the stories do the work. No lectures. No politics. Just the lived experience of those who served.
