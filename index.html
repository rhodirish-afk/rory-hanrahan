Writer · Publican · Oxfordshire

Rory Hanrahan

Originally from Ireland, I now live in Oxfordshire where I manage three village pubs with my wife. I write about politics, culture, pubs, Ireland, and the quiet erosion of common sense — mostly for The Spectator, with pieces also appearing in The Telegraph and Spiked.

My father was Sean O’Callaghan, the former IRA volunteer who turned informer. That history shapes how I see fanaticism, identity politics, and the value of ordinary decency.

Available for commissions and comment. myone_ie@hotmail.co.uk

Campaigns

I Stand With British Jews

A quiet campaign of solidarity. On Tisha B’Av 2026 I asked non-Jewish Britons to wear a simple badge bearing the Union Flag and the words “I Stand With British Jews”. No marches, no slogans about foreign policy — just a small act of neighbourly decency. Covered by The Times and Jewish News.

Veterans’ Story Hour

A new charity bringing British Armed Forces veterans into pubs, schools and community centres to share stories of service, resilience and comradeship. Supported by a Civic Future Action Grant. Starting in Oxfordshire with the aim of going national.
